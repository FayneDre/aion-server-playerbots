# Skill glossary: bard

The French client's names for the bard's skills and the group each one is in the data; [README.md](README.md) says where they
come from. Read on 15 November 2026 for the bard's playbook, which has no guide ([bard-plan.md](../plans/bard-plan.md)). The French client leaves a few
in English (Mild Echo, Loud Bang, Gust Requiem, Mosky Requiem) and gives two names twice: *Écho doux* is both Soothing Melody and Soothing Finale,
*Mélodie de la joie* both Joyous Carol and Rejuvenating Aria. A tester's French name is then settled by the group.
