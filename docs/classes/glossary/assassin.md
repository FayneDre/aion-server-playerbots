on 18 November 2026 for the assassin's playbook, which has no guide ([assassin-plan.md](../plans/assassin-plan.md)). The French client leaves a few
in English (Dash and Slash, Flurry, Signet Silence, Cyclone Slash, Aethertwisting), gives Shimmerbomb and Blinding Burst the same name, and names
Slayer Form after its last rank in the data, a test skill.
