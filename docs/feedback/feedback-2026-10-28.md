Status: points 3 and 4 handled (3 in the navigation backlog, 4 fixed, unverified in game); points 1 and 2 not explained yet, logs added.

A group in an instance at level 65, then a group in Draupnir Cave.

## 1. A templar that does not take the resurrection

A dead templar was cast a resurrection and stayed dead. The acceptance path (`BotDeath.onDeath`, then `bringBack`) reads right and shares
`revive()` with the walk back from the obelisk, which works. Not explained. Added: a log line when a bot is offered a resurrection, an error log
when coming back throws (the scheduler swallowed it and the bot was offered it again every tick), and the resurrection flag made `volatile` since the
caster's thread writes it and the bot's reads it. To read in the next log: "has been offered a resurrection" with no "accepted a resurrection" after it.

## 2. The gladiator switching stance in a loop after the resurrection

Slaughter and Defense Preparation are separate toggles (stacks `FI_BLADEMODE` and `FI_DEFENSEMODE`), switched by `decideAtRest` on whether a
templar of the group stands. A loop needs that answer to change again and again, for instance a templar raised at a third of its health and dead
again. Not confirmed. Added: each switch is logged with the group size and the tank-dead answer.

In the navigation backlog, item 7.

## 4. Trades with a companion

A bot never set its bag's size: `setCubeLimit()` runs as a player logs on and the bot's way in did not call it, so its bag held the 27 slots every
bag starts with, whatever it had bought or been given. A trade is refused when the receiver has fewer free slots than pieces offered. Fixed in
`PlayerBotEnterWorldService`: the limit is set as the bot enters the world, so it applies from the next time each bot spawns.
