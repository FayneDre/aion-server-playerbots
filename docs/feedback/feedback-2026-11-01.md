# Feedback of 1 November

Status: points 1 and 2 changed, not yet checked in game; point 3 not explained; points 4 and 5 waiting for the tester's notes and the log.

A spirit master in the Ruberein Wastes, then a group at the Alsig crossroads (Morheim).

## 1. The spirit does not fight, or without an animation

`BotServantAI.run()` put following before fighting: past `FOLLOW_DISTANCE` (5 m) from its master the servant walked back and did nothing else that
tick. Stepping towards an enemy carried it past 5 m, so it turned back, stepped out again, and never swung.

Changed: a fight outranks following while the servant is within `ENGAGE_DISTANCE` (25 m) of its master; the leash (50 m) still releases it first.
To check in game: a spirit master pulling a mob, the spirit swings and cast with its animation.

## 2. The spirit's walk is jerky

`BotServantMoveController.headFor` moved the destination every tick, but the start-move packet is only sent when the servant was standing: the
client walked the spirit to the first target, then it jumped to the server's position.

Changed: the packet is sent again once the target has moved more than 2 m from the last one announced. To check in game: the spirit following a
running master, no jumps.

## 3. The spirit seems to drain its master's health at rest

Not explained. The spirit casts a skill drawn at random from `npc_skills.xml`; "Spirit Absorption" (group `EL_UNSUMMON`) gives the master's health to
the spirit and may be what is drawn. To read: the skill ids the spirit casts while the master stands still.

## 4. A level 28 bot fighting level 34 monsters

The bot should not be in that zone at all. Quilemil (level 28, resident, home 2540 1790 in Morheim) died three times in forty minutes on the way to
the shop at 2672 2501, 700 m off: 2790 2214, 2772 2266, 2914 2393, through level 34 country (cruel worgs, citadel patrollers). The death rule moved
its home to 1722 1772 and it died again walking back. `BotVendorManager.findVendor` already bounded the trip (800 m) and dropped a shop with three
deaths within 150 m of it, but a death on the road is far from the shop and counted for nothing.

Changed: a shop is dropped when the straight line to it, tested every 40 m, crosses a hunting ground worth more than the bot's level plus 4
(`crossesDangerousGround`, `BotPlaces.groundLevelAt`, which answers 0 where no ground claims the spot). The line is a guess at the walked route.
To check: the log's "heads to a shop to sell" no longer ends in deaths on the road; a bag that never sells because every shop is refused is the cost.

## 5. Bots caught on fences, benches and small obstacles, many indoors

Two `//bot note` lines, "bug collision", at Alsig (2479 1799 96 and 2481 1781 95). The planner finds the walk to the tester ordinary (24 m for 20 m
in a line, ground that leads somewhere), so the mesh does not see what stops a bot: a vendor's counter, then a fence, says the tester. To look at with
`NavmeshTool 220020000 slice` at both spots ([verification-tools.md](../navigation/verification-tools.md)).

Looked at on 1 November: the counter is `pr_l_table_01a`, whose top the mesh counts as ground (z 96.15) whatever the decor rule says; adding "table" and
"counter" to the structure names changed nothing at the spot and was undone. The tester does not mind a bot hopping an obstacle, only a bot pressed
against one that then vanishes (the rescue's lift home).

Changed: `BotMoveController.checkProgress` hops a bot that has made no headway for 2 s (`hopOver`): up to 3.5 m ahead on mesh floor within 1.2 m of its
height, with the jump emotion, twice at most per stall, then the give up as before. Logged as "hops over what holds it". To check in game: a bot
at a counter or fence crosses it instead of standing, and the rescue's "so it is the spot that leads nowhere" lines become rarer near Alsig.

## 6. A bot resting with its wings out

One gladiator, seen twice at the Alsig obelisk, sitting then standing, wings out. Neither the log of 1 November nor the archives of the night before hold
a flight or a glide of it (449 lines read), so the ambient ledge glide of 31 October is not shown to be the cause. `Creature.isFlying()` counts a
flying body that is resting as not flying, which would hide such a state from every check of the module.

Changed: `BotRestManager.sitDown` folds the wings of a bot the engine holds as flying or gliding with no flight of ours behind it, and logs "sits with
its wings out". To read: that line, with the state it prints, tells which path left the flag set.
