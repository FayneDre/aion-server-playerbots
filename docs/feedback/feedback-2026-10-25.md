# Feedback of 25 October

Status: all six points built and unit tested where a test can say anything, shipped in `patch-2026-10-25-2`; the cleric's heals, wings and power shards validated in game on 27 October, the rest not watched yet.

The tester's notes on the cleric (thresholds corrected, signs that were inverted in the guide) and five points from a session in Theobomos.

## The cleric's heals

The guide's thresholds, corrected by the tester: on the tank, the lowest threshold first (Flash of Healing under 30 %, Flash of Recovery under 50 %,
Healing Grace under 60 %); for two members hurt, the area heals ahead of the tank's own unless the tank is under 50 %; the heal over time on the tank
from the first moment of a fight; the two seven second channels cast. Details in [cleric-plan.md](../classes/cleric-plan.md).

The heal over time never reached the tank because it asked for the tank to be under 95 %, and a tank is at full health when a fight begins.

## Bots brought to the player on an invitation

A bot that accepts a group or alliance invitation from a player more than 50 m away, or on another map, is put beside the player
(`BotRecruiter.bringInvited`, through `BotInstances.moveBeside`). It is kept as a recruit with no offer, so it goes home when it leaves the group.
Left alone: a recruit, already brought; a player or a bot in an instance, which `BotInstances` follows; a dead one; one whose acceptance did not
put it in the group. A companion is brought but not sent home afterwards: it goes back to its owner.

## Power shards

`BotPowerShards`: bought with the flasks (a shortage of shards is a reason for the trip, at half of the 1000 carried, or 200 for a light purse, with a wait of an hour after a shop
that sold none), worn in the right place, and in the left one too for a two handed weapon or two weapons, on the bot's tick (after a purchase, and
every 30 s between fights), switched on as a fight starts and off as it ends. Only the merchants' shards
(`battery_10` to `battery_70`); the shelves stop at Premium (level 50), so a bot of 60 uses those. The engine burns them and boosts magic as well.

## Wings

None of the 1950 bots wore any. Only the wings a merchant sells for kinah, as the tester asked: not those of a quest, an instance, a reward or the
abyss (`BotGearSources.isSoldForKinah`, trade lists of the ordinary kind). That is three pairs per side, at 30 (three qualities), 40 and 50 (two
each), so wings from level 30; a bot of 50 to 65 wears the level 50 ones. The side is read from the model (`light` or `dark`), the quality kept under
the gear's ceiling for the level. Handed over like the rest of the gear, not paid for. Renewed when the level changes (`renewWings`).

## The cleric that seemed to jump to the monster

A client stops a body that casts. The server holds the walk during the cast and resumes it after without telling anyone, since the destination has
not changed; the client kept the cleric where it cast and the next packet, often at the fight's end, showed it jump. The step after a cast, or after
an instant spell cast on the way, now sends the walk again. This is the explanation the report fits, not one that was seen: watch a group's cleric.

## Resting in lava

The engine knows which ground hurts (`TerrainZoneCollisionMaterialActor` for the terrain, `MaterialZoneHandler` for a mesh), and a bot did not ask.
`HarmfulGround`: before resting, a bot on ground whose material casts a hostile skill on it walks to the nearest ground that does not, up to three
times. The terrain can be asked of any spot; a mesh's material only of a body standing in it.

## What to watch

The cleric's heal over time on an unhurt templar, and a channel landing at its top stage. A bot invited from far away arriving, then going home when
it leaves. Wings drawn on bots from 30, and power shards burning in a fight (the shard count goes down, the weapon glows). A group's cleric no longer
jumping. A melee bot fighting the hoverstones of the Sulfur Swamp stepping out of the lava to rest.
