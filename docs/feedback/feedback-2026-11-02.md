# Feedback of 2 November

Status: done (the upstream fix, applied as is; the user considers it settled).

## 1. Fall damage on the Sanctum lift (Artisans' Hall to Protectors' Hall)

Reproduced by several testers, never seen before the merge of 28 October: standing still on the descending lift costs health at regular intervals;
walking about on it costs nothing.

Cause: not the bots. Upstream's "Aligned falling with retail" (`82a5e0c`, merged on 28 October) counts a fall from the last grounded height, and
the lift's move packets carry the `FALL` and `VEHICLE` masks. Standing still, the client sends few packets, so each one lands more than 10 m below
the last: damage. Walking, the packets are close together and each stays under 10 m.

Changed: `CM_MOVE` treats a `FALL` packet that also carries `VEHICLE` as a new start of the fall (`restartFalling`), not as a fall in progress.
This is upstream's own fix (`9b171d2`), taken as it is so the next upstream merge brings nothing to resolve. To check in game: ride the lift down
standing still, then moving (no damage); drop more than 10 m off a ledge (still hurts); more than 50 m (still kills).
