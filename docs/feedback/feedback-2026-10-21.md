# Tester feedback of 21 October: the capitals

Status: points 1 and 4 built and tested on 23 October, not watched in game. Point 2 built the same day. Point 3 became the capital visitors, built and watched in Pandaemonium on 24 October ([capital-life-plan.md](../capitals/capital-life-plan.md)).

## 1. A bot standing in the air in Pandaemonium

Aelonra, a level 47 gladiator living by the Great Temple (home 1479 1362 177), stood at 1462 1398 **204.6** from 20 October 13:48 until the
report: 27 m above the floor. The mesh has floors there at 177.5 and 184.8 and a blocked surface at 207.9, nothing at 204.6
(`NavmeshTool 120010000 1462.3 1398.3`). It got there walking to a shop on a three waypoint route, and how it climbed is not explained.

What kept it there is:

- **In game**, it asked for a route about 180 times an hour and gave up on each one, and the anti-stuck rescue never came. The rescue
  counts refused moves, and a move whose route is searched on another thread answers "on its way" first, which resets the count.
  `BotDay` and `BotErrands` gave up on the destinations one after the other, and nothing counted that.
- **At every start**, the saved spot came back. `standOnGround` looks for ground within a few metres of height. It found none and read
  that as "no mesh for this map".

The changes:

- `PlayerBotAI.destinationRefused`: six give-ups in a row from the same spot (within 3 m), away from home, trigger the rescue. The rescue
  then lifts the bot even when a player sees it (`freeItself(isolated)`), because a body standing in the air is a worse sight than a blink.
- The rescue's "already there" test is now in three dimensions: a ledge straight above the front door is not the front door.
- `PlayerBotEnterWorldService.landFromTheAir`: a bot saved with no ground near it is put on the floor below it, if that floor leads home,
  and at home otherwise. Over the void (no floor under it), it is left alone. A home that is itself in the air is replaced by that floor:
  on the first start, Heirtank (templar, Poeta) stood at its home, 1402 m above the ground, and the rule sent it back there.
- Deployed on 23 October. At the first start Aelonra came down at its home (1471 1355 177), then walked to its stall and opened its store.

## 2. Bot stores badly spread in Pandaemonium

Pandaemonium has no markets of its own, so stalls are taken round the brokers and warehouses (`BotStores.aroundTheCounters`). Over every log,
the Asmodian stalls fell in three spots: about 80 at 1340-1360 1470-1510, 20 at 1290-1310 1480-1500 and 15 at 1170-1190 1530-1550. The fix is
markets for Pandaemonium, as Sanctum has: four, placed by the user with `//bot note` on 23 October, each checked walkable and joined to the others
with `NavmeshTool` (see [bot-stores-plan.md](../capitals/bot-stores-plan.md)).

## 4. Class gear stores selling other classes' pieces

Fenulik's "Équipement gladiateur 31-41" held a cloth headband, a leather hat and two shields. Helmets all share the group `HEAD`, so the
data gives them no armour type, and a shield weighs nothing either. Both went through the "heaviest armour only" filter.

- `BotGearFit.armourWeight` reads a helmet's type from its model name (`pl_`, `ch_`, `lt_`, `rb_`, `cl_`). The `ac_` hats any class wears
  keep no type.
- `BotGearFit.carriesAShield`: templar and cleric, and the starting classes they come from. Stores stock shields for those classes only.
- The same rules apply to what bots wear (`isRightArmour`): a gladiator no longer goes out in a leather hat or with a shield. Pieces
  that no longer fit a store's theme are not put back on its shelf after a restart, and stay in the bag as loot.

## The first look at the stores, 23 October

- **Every class store sold plate.** Every piece declares every class, so the filter kept the heaviest armour the store found, plate for all.
  `BotGearFit.heaviestLearnedBy` reads the class's heaviest mastery from the skill tree instead: plate for gladiator and templar, chain for
  cleric, chanter and aethertech, leather for assassin, ranger and gunner, robe for sorcerer, spiritmaster and bard.
- **Too few stores.** Five merchants in ten on 6 to 10 hour shifts gave about 16 open in Sanctum and 21 in Pandaemonium. Now eight in ten.
- **Too packed.** Stalls 6 m apart instead of 3, markets 18 to 22 m round instead of 12 to 15. Sanctum then held 29 and gets a sixth
  market round the Elyos Square, placed by the user.
- **Sanctum still short** at 26, Pandaemonium full: a town's stores depended on how many of its residents happened to be on shift. Stand-ins
  (merchants off shift filling in) were tried and swapped stalls in a loop, 153 times in seven minutes. Replaced by a rota: 44 merchants of a
  town on shift at every hour, the window going round them once a day.
  Then stalls became a fixed grid per market, and the rota skips merchants with no way to the markets. Fifteen minutes after a restart:
  Sanctum 37 stores, Pandaemonium 39.

## The "merged server" warning at character selection

With `gameserver.character.creation.mode = 1` (both factions on one account), the client shows a caution about merged servers at every
character selection. Telling the client mode 0 while the server allowed both was tried on 23 October: the warning stayed. The client raises it
from the character list itself, and no packet sent before the selection carries anything else to turn it off. Not fixable from the server.
