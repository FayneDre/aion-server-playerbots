# Tester feedback of 20 October: group play in Eltnen

Status: the five points of 20 October deployed on 23 October; those of 19 October (end of file) deployed the same day (`aef68a42d`); all validated in game on 27 October.

One session, 12:47 to 13:33 (`archived/2026-10-20 10.18 to 2026-10-20 13.32.zip`): player Sithis with Fenadric (templar), Rasemra (cleric) and
Fenonwyn, around the Kaidan Mine. Each point below is the report, what the log showed, the cause and the change.

## 1. A dead member walks back to the group before resting, and dies on the way

Fenonwyn revived at the obelisk (943 2205) at 12:55:51 on a quarter of its health, set off for the group 300 m away and died at 12:56:54, halfway.
`mustCatchUp` forbade a member to rest whenever it was more than 5 m from its place, a rule written for a member a few steps behind.

Now a member back from the obelisk (soul sickness) and more than 40 m from its group (`CATCH_UP_UNRESTED`) walks off the obelisk and rests to the
engage threshold first. Nearer than that, or without the sickness, it still catches up without sitting down.

## 2. The cleric stopped healing, the templar died, and the player was not healed after

From 13:11:19 to 13:11:37, during the elite *kamtak the destroyer*, Rasemra logged "starts attacking veteran kaidan scout" 18 times and cast nothing;
Fenadric died at 13:11:37. The same loop is in the log at 13:17:33 and 13:17:53. Two rules read the same monster two ways: `findAttacker` counted the
scout, which hated the cleric because she had rooted it; `healerHasNoSurplus` let the fight go because the scout was not aimed at her. Each tick
started the fight, the fight loop dropped it, and neither the decision tick's heal nor the fight loop's ever ran.

Now a group's healer counts as an attacker only what is aimed at it, the fight loop's rule, and it heals a mate in danger (`HEALER_DANGER_PERCENT`)
before answering its own attacker.

## 3. Bots glide strangely

Fenonwyn and Fenadric copied the player's glides at 13:19:21 and 13:19:39. Following a player's glide is wanted (a shortcut through the air); what
was wrong is that the bots were drawn standing in the air. The glide legs went out as `POSITION | MANUAL` without the `GLIDE` bit a gliding
client's `CM_MOVE` carries, so a client drew a body with no wings. The mask now carries `GLIDE` with `GlideFlag.NONE` while the controller glides,
which also concerns the glides of the open-world glide links.

## 4 and 5. The player takes a flight or a teleporter, and the bots do not follow

At 13:29:17 Sithis took the flight from the obelisk. The three bots asked for a walking route every second for a minute, were rescued to their own
homes at 13:30:23 (Rasemra to 581 183), and Fenonwyn died at 13:30:53 walking towards a leader 1000 m away. `BotInstances` covered instances only.

`BotTransports` (new): while the player is on a flight transporter or a windstream the members neither walk nor fly after them (their anchor stays
where the flight left); when the player lands, or after a teleport (100 m further than a player covers at 20 m/s in the time between two looks, or
another map outside instances), each member is put beside them with `BotInstances.moveBeside`. A member dead at the time is taken along once revived. The anti-stuck
rescue no longer sends a member of a player's group home, at any distance on the same map: it is put beside its player.

## How to verify, in one session

With a cleric, a templar and one more bot: die far from the group and watch the bot rest before walking back (1); fight an elite with adds and
watch the cleric's heals (2); glide off a ledge and watch the wings (3); take a flight and a teleporter (4, 5). Then:
`grep -a "joins .* after\|waits for .* who has taken a flight\|glides after its leader\|starts attacking" server_console.log`.

## The report of 19 October, on the patches since 16 October

**Recruiting works; the setting was documented in the wrong file.** `form_anywhere` lives in `config/main/group.properties`; `mygs.properties` is
an optional override that a fresh install does not have. The three mentions in `patch-notes-2026-10.md` now say both.

**Ranged bots out of sight of their target**, typically on stairs: in reach, every cast refused for want of a line of sight, the fight given up after
20 s. `BotStations.spotFor` now asks the ground and, for a ranged bot, the enemy's line of sight of each candidate, nearest first
(`CombatSpots.spotFor` takes the test; the old signature is kept for the tests); a ranged bot out of sight no longer counts as placed; and
`comesIntoSight` walks any bot but a group's healer towards a target in reach and out of sight until it sees it.

**Flight-only buffs** (the guide: "buffs de vitesse de vol ou de regen de temps de vol"). `BotSkillManager.isFlightOnly` recognises a skill that does
something for flight and nothing a body on the ground would miss; `FlightBuffDataTest` holds the six it finds against the data (Soaring Sonnet,
Swiftwing, Strengthen Wings, Splendor of Flight, Heal Wings, Fuel Reserves). None is cast on the ground any more, and `tryFlightBuff`, called on
take-off after the class's own `onTakeOff`, casts the gladiator's and the songweaver's, which nothing cast before; a refill of flight points waits
for the bar to be under half.
