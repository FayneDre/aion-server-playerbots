# Economy: what is left in detail

Status: in progress: the broker validated in game (15 November), its prices and goods left to adjust; bot shops in the world merged into the
travelling merchants (lot C1, 15 November); gathering and crafting left out by decision.

Moved from the roadmap on 7 November 2026, when it became one page by theme: the detail of what is left, kept here with its numbers. The
order and the status of each item are in [roadmap.md](../roadmap.md).

## Selling, the broker, shops

**Selling works**: 37 sales in 38 trips on the night of 2 October, keepers found among the living npcs, refused shops remembered, trade within 8 m,
and the keeper's own floor where it stands a storey away (17 October, Teminon), unless that floor is a pocket behind a counter, where a bot
walked in and could not get out (18 October, the Verteron citadel). Since 18 October a trip walks to the keeper it chose, not the nearest one
seen, and to the floor in front of it rather than its feet: trips given up 269 -> 91 in the first 23 minutes. No shop on another layer of the map is gone to; fortress shops are
kept, by decision. A player's own bot never sells, buys, dresses from, identifies or binds what is in its bag ([bot-gear.md](../gear/how-it-works.md)).

The **broker** followed, a house the server stocks ([auction-house-plan.md](plans/auction-house-plan.md)), validated on 15 November. **Bot shops** are
the 40 stores of each capital; those in the world are the travelling merchants of lot C1 ([living-world-plan.md](../population/plans/living-world-plan.md)).
**Gathering and crafting** by bots are left out by decision (15 November).

## Gear

- **Gear does not keep up.** Bots are dressed at creation (enchanted, socketed, with stigmas) and wear what they loot; only wings are renewed with
  the level (25 October). Left as it is by decision (15 November): bots neither seek, buy nor enchant their own gear. Existing gunners keep their cannon until `//bot regear all` (two pistols since 17 October).
